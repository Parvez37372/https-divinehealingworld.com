Understanding the Practice

What Are the Akashic Records?

The Akashic Records are described in certain spiritual traditions as an energetic or spiritual record of the soul's experiences, lessons, and journey.

An Akashic Records Reading is a spiritual practice intended to explore questions about your life, relationships, personal development, and spiritual growth. It offers an opportunity to reflect on experiences that may feel repetitive or difficult to understand and consider their possible meaning within your personal journey.

Each session is unique, shaped by your questions, intentions, and the areas of life you wish to explore.
