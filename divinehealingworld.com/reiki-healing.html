Understanding Reiki

What Is Reiki Healing?

Reiki is a Japanese complementary wellness practice. The word is commonly explained through two Japanese terms: Rei, meaning universal or spiritual, and Ki, meaning life energy.

During a Reiki session, a practitioner places their hands lightly on or just above the body, following the recipient's comfort and consent. Some sessions may also be offered without physical touch. The experience is generally quiet and calming, allowing the person to rest and focus on their own sense of well-being.

Reiki is based on a traditional belief in the flow of life energy. Scientific evidence has not established that this energy can be measured or that Reiki can treat or cure medical conditions. People may nevertheless choose it as a complementary relaxation practice.

In simple words: Reiki is a gentle, hands-on or hands-near wellness practice that creates time for relaxation and self-reflection. Your experience may be different from someone else's.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Reiki Healing

Some helpful information before you decide whether a session is right for you.

Is Reiki a medical treatment?

No. Reiki is considered a complementary wellness practice, not a medical treatment. It should not replace medical advice, diagnosis, medication, or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.

Do I need to believe in Reiki for a session?

You do not need to follow a particular belief system. You can simply approach the session as a quiet wellness experience and decide whether it feels comfortable for you.

Does Reiki involve physical touch?

Some practitioners use light touch, while others work with their hands just above the body. Your consent and comfort should always be discussed before the session.

How will I feel after a Reiki session?

Experiences differ. Some people describe feeling relaxed, while others may notice no particular change. No specific result can be guaranteed.

Can Reiki cure an illness or health condition?

Reiki has not been established as a cure for illnesses or medical conditions. Please continue appropriate medical care and speak with your healthcare professional about health concerns.
